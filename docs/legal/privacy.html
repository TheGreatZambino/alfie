01Overview

Alfie helps you track your finances, workouts, and nutrition in one app. It's built and maintained by a single independent developer — not a company with a data-monetization business model. The short version: your financial, workout, and nutrition data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account. Nothing you enter into Alfie is sold, and it isn't shared with anyone for advertising.

02Data you provide

When you use Alfie, you may enter:

  • Finance data — income, bills, spending categories, transactions, and savings/investment balances you enter manually.
  • Workout data — exercise templates, logged sets, and workout sessions you create.
  • Nutrition data — food entries, either typed in, searched, or scanned via barcode.
  • Account basics — your name and a stable identifier from Sign in with Apple, used only to keep your account signed in on this device.

None of this is sent to a server we operate — Alfie has no backend of its own. It's stored on your device and synced across your devices using your personal iCloud account, exactly like Apple's own apps (Notes, Reminders) do.

03Health & fitness data

If you enable it, Alfie reads your step count and logged cardio workouts from Apple Health (HealthKit) to credit them toward your weekly goals. Alfie does not write anything back to Health.

HealthKit data is never used for advertising or analytics, and is never sold to data brokers or advertising platforms — this applies even if you also see ads elsewhere in the app, and it's a commitment independent of what any other section of this policy says about analytics or advertising.

You control this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Alfie.

04Camera

Alfie uses your camera only to scan food barcodes when you tap the scan button in Nutrition. The camera feed is processed on-device to read the barcode; Alfie does not save photos or video, and nothing from the camera is transmitted anywhere.

05Face ID / device lock

If you turn on "Require Face ID" in Settings, Alfie asks iOS to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before showing your data after the app is backgrounded. This check happens entirely on your device through Apple's LocalAuthentication framework — Alfie never receives or stores any biometric data itself.

06Storage & iCloud sync

Alfie stores your data using Apple's SwiftData framework, backed by your personal iCloud account (CloudKit private database). That means:

  • Your data syncs between your own devices signed into the same iCloud account.
  • We — the developer of Alfie — cannot see, access, or download your data. It never passes through a server we control.
  • Apple's iCloud terms and security practices govern that sync layer; see Apple's own privacy documentation for details.

07Analytics

Alfie uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand broad usage patterns — for example, how many devices logged a workout this week. Signals are tied to an anonymous, device-derived identifier, not to your name, account, or any of your finance/health/nutrition content. TelemetryDeck cannot be used to identify you personally.

08Advertising

If you haven't subscribed to Alfie Plus, Alfie shows banner ads served by Google AdMob — but only on the Nutrition and Workouts tabs. Finances and Overview never show ads, regardless of subscription status.

Google's AdMob SDK may use device or advertising identifiers to select and measure ads. On first launch of an ad-eligible screen, iOS may prompt you for App Tracking Transparency permission; declining still shows ads, just less targeted ones. See Google's advertising policy for how Google itself handles that data.

09Subscriptions & payment

Alfie Plus ($3.99/month) removes ads and is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple — Alfie never sees or stores your payment details. Alfie only receives confirmation from Apple's StoreKit that your subscription is active, which it uses to hide ads.

11Retention & deletion

Your data stays in your private iCloud account for as long as you keep the app installed and signed in. To delete it:

  • Delete individual entries directly within Alfie, or
  • Sign out (Settings → Sign out) to remove local access on that device, or
  • Delete the app, then remove its iCloud data via iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Alfie, which permanently deletes it from iCloud.

12Children's privacy

Alfie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

13Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the effective date at the top of this page will update. Continued use of Alfie after a change means you accept the revised policy.
